HOW TO PLAY

Three in a row.

Take turns placing X and O on the board. The first to line up three marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins. If every square fills and nobody has three, it’s a draw.

Choose a mode above. In computer mode, pick a difficulty and whether you want to move first. Same-device mode passes the turn between two players. Different-device mode lets you send a single invite link; the other player opens it to join.
